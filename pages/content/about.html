How it began

From a Kitchen to Your Skin

It started with a simple frustration — the inability to find skincare products that were truly natural, honestly made, and actually worked. Store shelves were full of products claiming to be "herbal" but packed with synthetic chemicals.

So we went back to the roots. To the knowledge passed down through generations in Indian households — the turmeric packs, the neem soap bars, the coconut oil hair treatments that truly worked.

Today, every SkinMatters product is still handcrafted in small batches using cold-process techniques, traditional recipes, and ethically sourced organic ingredients from across India.
