The Ancient Indian 4-Step Skincare Routine You Need to Try

SkinMatters Team April 15, 2025 5 min read

Long before serums and retinols became buzzwords, Indian women maintained luminous skin with a deceptively simple philosophy: use what grows in nature, apply it consistently, and treat skin as a reflection of overall health.

The Ayurvedic approach to skincare is built on four pillars — Cleanse, Exfoliate, Moisturise, and Nourish — each corresponding to a specific skin need and best served by specific natural ingredients.

"Nature has an ingredient for every skin concern. The challenge is not finding what works, but learning to trust it."

Step 1: Cleanse — Remove Without Stripping

The first mistake most people make is using a harsh surfactant-based cleanser that strips the skin's natural acid mantle. Ayurvedic cleansing uses mild, plant-based agents that clean while preserving moisture.

Best natural cleansers:

  • Aloe Vera Soap — Gentle, anti-inflammatory, ideal for sensitive and acne-prone skin.
  • Charcoal Soap — Activated charcoal draws out toxins without over-drying.
  • Neem + Turmeric Soap — Classical Ayurvedic combination for antimicrobial action.

Step 2: Exfoliate — Reveal Fresh Skin

Exfoliation removes dead skin cells and stimulates cell turnover. Ayurveda traditionally uses ubtan — a paste of chickpea flour, turmeric, and botanical powders — as a gentle weekly scrub.

Modern natural exfoliants include herbal face packs with multani mitti (Fuller's Earth), rose petal powder, and rice flour. These absorb excess oil, refine pores, and brighten skin tone without micro-tears.

Pro tip: Exfoliate no more than 2-3 times per week. Over-exfoliating weakens the skin barrier and causes more harm than good.

Step 3: Moisturise — Lock in Hydration

The Ayurvedic approach to moisturising goes beyond water-based creams. Oils and butter are used to seal in hydration at the cellular level. The right oil depends on your skin type (dosha) — but most Indians do well with lightweight options like aloe vera gel, jojoba oil, or kokum butter.

  • Vata skin (dry, thin): Sesame oil, almond oil
  • Pitta skin (sensitive, redness): Coconut oil, sandalwood
  • Kapha skin (oily, congested): Neem oil, light jojoba

Step 4: Nourish — Target Your Skin Concerns

The final step is treatment — applying a targeted botanical serum or overnight oil to address specific concerns like pigmentation, fine lines, or dark circles. This is where plant actives like vitamin C (from amla), bakuchiol, and kumkumadi oil shine.

Consistency matters more than complexity. A simple 4-step routine done every day will always outperform an elaborate 10-step routine done occasionally.

Building Your Routine

You don't need 10 products. A well-chosen set of 4-5 products used consistently will transform your skin over 4-8 weeks. Start with the basics — a good natural soap, a weekly pack, a light moisturiser, and a nourishing oil — and build from there.
